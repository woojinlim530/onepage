Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

One Page is designed to help you reflect on your thoughts privately. This policy describes how the app handles journal entries and related information.

1. Information you provide

You may enter personal reflections, responses to prompts, and follow-up journal entries. The app is designed to save these entries locally on your iPhone and does not require an account for its core journaling functions.

2. AI-assisted reflection

On supported devices, One Page uses Apple's on-device intelligence capabilities for AI-assisted reflection. One Page does not operate its own server to receive your journal entries for AI processing. Availability depends on your device and Apple Intelligence settings.

3. Collection, advertising, and tracking

One Page is designed without third-party advertising or behavioral advertising tracking. The developer does not intentionally collect journal entries on developer-operated servers or sell journal content. Information may be processed by Apple and your device according to Apple's applicable terms and system features.

4. Storage and deletion

Journal entries are designed to be stored locally within the app's storage. You can manage entries using the controls provided in the app. Deleting the app may remove locally stored entries, though device backups or restored data may be governed by your iOS settings.

5. Security

One Page relies on iOS app sandboxing and operating system protections. No digital storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children

One Page is a general wellness tool, not specifically directed at children. Parents and guardians should consider whether its reflection features are appropriate for younger users.

7. Medical disclaimer

One Page is intended for personal reflection and general wellness. It does not provide medical diagnosis, therapy, or emergency intervention. For medical or mental health concerns, consult an appropriate qualified professional.

8. Updates

We may revise this Privacy Policy when app functionality or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact
