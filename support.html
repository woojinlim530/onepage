One Page Support

Questions about One Page? Find answers below or contact us directly.

What is One Page?

One Page is a personal reflection journal inspired by Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT). It offers structured prompts to help you explore your thoughts and consider different perspectives.

How does Apple Intelligence work?

On compatible devices where Apple Intelligence is available and enabled, One Page can help guide reflection through AI-assisted questions. Feature availability depends on device compatibility and system settings.

Can I write without AI?

Yes. One Page offers a manual writing option so you can reflect and save your thoughts without AI assistance.

Can I revisit previous entries?

Yes. You can reopen saved entries. Follow-up functionality lets you continue reflecting on previous topics when available.

Where are my entries saved?

One Page is designed to store your reflections locally in the app on your iPhone. See our Privacy Policy for more information.

Is One Page therapy?

No. One Page is a self-reflection and general wellness tool, not a medical device or a substitute for professional mental health care.

I need urgent help. What should I do?

One Page does not provide crisis support. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services. For urgent emotional support, contact a crisis service in your area.
